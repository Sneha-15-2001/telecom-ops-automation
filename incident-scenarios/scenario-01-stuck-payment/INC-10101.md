# INC-10101 — Order stuck in PAYMENT_PENDING (payment recorded, never validated)

- **Status:** Open (P2)
- **Reported:** customer care — "customer paid but order not moving"
- **Symptom:** order shows `PAYMENT_PENDING` hours after the customer paid via UPI.
- **Suspected area:** order-service payment validation step never ran.

## 🧑‍💼 What the reporter says

Yesterday afternoon the customer placed an order on the storefront and paid
₹299 via UPI. The payment gateway debited the money and our system recorded
the payment attempt — then nothing. The back-office operator saw
`PAYMENT_PENDING`, assumed "gateway delay", and closed the ticket. In reality
the `payment/validate` step was never invoked (callback lost / manual step
missed), so the order never advanced to fulfillment while the customer was
already charged.

## 🔧 Engineering notes

### Timeline recovered from the system

1. `ORDER_CREATED` — order placed with 1 item (see `GET /api/orders/{id}/history`)
2. `STATUS_CHANGED CREATED → VALIDATED` — customer + item checks passed
3. `STATUS_CHANGED VALIDATED → PAYMENT_PENDING` — submitted, awaiting payment
4. `PAYMENT_RECORDED` — UPI attempt logged, status PENDING
5. **Silence** — no `PAYMENT_VALIDATED`, no `ORDER_FAILED`; the trail just stops

### How to reproduce

```bash
bash incident-scenarios/scenario-01-stuck-payment/setup.sh
# note the printed orderId/orderNumber/correlationId
```

### Evidence to collect

- `GET /api/orders/{id}/status` → `PAYMENT_PENDING`
- `GET /api/orders/{id}/payments` → one row, status `PENDING`
- `GET /api/orders/{id}/history` → `PAYMENT_RECORDED` present, **no** `PAYMENT_VALIDATED`
- Logs (`scripts/search-logs.sh <correlationId>`): `PAYMENT_RECORDED` with no matching
  `PAYMENT_VALIDATED` — the validation step was skipped, not failed.
