# INC-10109 — Promotion silently vanished after order modification

- **Status:** Open (P3)
- **Reported:** customer complaint — "my discount disappeared and the price went up"
- **Symptom:** order had `FESTIVE10` (payable ₹269.10); after adding one more
  line, promo is `null` and payable jumped to the full amount.
- **Suspected area:** `modify` clears `promoCode`/`discountAmount` by design
  (stale discounts must not survive item changes) but tells nobody — no history
  entry names the promo removal, no re-apply reminder.

## 🧑‍💼 What the reporter says

The customer applied a festive promo, then called care to add a second line to
the same order. The agent modified the items; the backend correctly recomputed
totals but silently dropped the promotion (by design — discounts are computed
off old items). The new payable was higher than quoted, the customer paid it,
then saw the old quote and complained about overcharging.

## 🔧 Engineering notes

### Timeline recovered from the system

1. `ORDER_CREATED` → `PROMOTION_APPLIED FESTIVE10 discount 29.90`
2. `ORDER_MODIFIED` — items replaced, promo cleared (comment doesn't mention it)
3. Customer pays the higher amount; complaint follows

### Evidence to collect

- `GET /api/orders/{id}` → `promoCode: null`, `discountAmount: 0`, higher payable
- History shows `PROMOTION_APPLIED` then `ORDER_MODIFIED`, with no promo-removal entry
- Logs: no `PROMOTION_*` event at modify time
