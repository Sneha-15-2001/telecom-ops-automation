# INC-10105 — Customer got the same ORDER_COMPLETED SMS twice

- **Status:** Open (P3)
- **Reported:** customer complaint — duplicate SMS for one order.
- **Symptom:** two `ORDER_COMPLETED` notifications for order 2 within seconds.
- **Suspected area:** fulfillment retried `notify` without idempotency (no dedupe key on event+order).

## 🧑‍💼 What the reporter says

Yesterday the fulfillment saga's notify step timed out on its first call — but
the notification had actually been queued. The saga retried the step, queuing
a second identical `ORDER_COMPLETED` SMS. Both were later sent, and the
customer received the same "order complete" message twice within a minute and
complained about spam. The root defect: `notify` has no idempotency key on
(order + event), so a retried step duplicates instead of deduping.
