# INC-10104 — Customer never got the ORDER_COMPLETED SMS

- **Status:** Open (P3)
- **Reported:** customer care — "order completed but no SMS received"
- **Symptom:** notification `FAILED`, `lastError` = `SIMULATED_PROVIDER_REJECT`.
- **Suspected area:** recipient number rejected by downstream gateway; retry never attempted.

## 🧑‍💼 What the reporter says

The order completed successfully yesterday and the system queued the
`ORDER_COMPLETED` SMS — but the customer's number belonged to a range the
downstream gateway refuses. Delivery attempt #1 came back `550 recipient
rejected`, the notification flipped to `FAILED`, and since nobody retried (no
auto-retry exists), the customer was never told their service was active. They
called care asking "is my order done?" while the system said COMPLETED.

## 🔧 Engineering notes

### Timeline recovered from the system

1. `NOTIFICATION_QUEUED` — ORDER_COMPLETED SMS, status PENDING
2. `NOTIFICATION_FAILED` — attempt #1, `550 recipient rejected (simulated)`
3. **Silence** — no retry, notification left FAILED


### Evidence to collect

- `GET /api/notifications/{id}` → `FAILED`, attempts = 1
- `GET /api/notifications/{id}/attempts` → provider `550 recipient rejected (simulated)`
- Logs: `NOTIFICATION_FAILED`, no `NOTIFICATION_SENT`, no retry.
