# INC-10106 — Order auto-failed: customer is SUSPENDED

- **Status:** Open (P2)
- **Reported:** sales — "order died instantly, customer says account is fine"
- **Symptom:** order goes `CREATED → FAILED` at validation with
  `CUSTOMER_INVALID: [CUSTOMER_NOT_ACTIVE:SUSPENDED]`.
- **Suspected area:** customer status vs. sales promise mismatch — is the suspension
  correct, or is stale customer data killing good orders?

## 🧑‍💼 What the reporter says

CUS-DEMO003 was suspended weeks ago after a fraud flag on the corporate
account — a correct, audited status change. Yesterday sales, unaware of the
suspension, promised the customer a new connection and created the order.
Validation called customer-service, which truthfully reported `SUSPENDED`,
and the order auto-failed in milliseconds. Sales blames "the system killing
orders"; master-data blames "sales not checking eligibility first".

## 🔧 Engineering notes

### Timeline recovered from the system

1. (weeks ago) `CUSTOMER_STATUS_CHANGED → SUSPENDED` — fraud flag, corporate account
2. (yesterday) `ORDER_CREATED` — sales opened the order anyway
3. `STATUS_CHANGED CREATED → VALIDATING`, then `ORDER_FAILED` —
   `CUSTOMER_INVALID: [CUSTOMER_NOT_ACTIVE:SUSPENDED]`
