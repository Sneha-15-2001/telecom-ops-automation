# INC-10114 — Advertised promo code rejected as expired

- **Status:** Open (P3)
- **Reported:** customer care — "customer has a flyer with 5% off, checkout refuses it"
- **Symptom:** applying `EXPIRED5` fails: `outside its validity window`.
- **Suspected area:** campaign ended in the catalog but flyers/SMS went out late —
  master-data vs marketing timing mismatch (cousin of INC-10106's stale-data theme).

## 🧑‍💼 What the reporter says

The customer brought last month's festival flyer promising 5% off and wants it
honored. Checkout refuses the code. The flyer is genuine — the campaign simply
ended two weeks ago and nobody told the stores. Care needs to know: honor
manually, or hold the line?

## 🔧 Engineering notes

1. `ORDER_CREATED`, no promo
2. `promotion/apply EXPIRED5` → `400 outside its validity window`
3. `promotion.valid_to` in DB is weeks in the past; `active` still true (stale flag)
